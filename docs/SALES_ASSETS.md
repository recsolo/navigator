**You're paying for tools that overlap.**
→ Navigator cuts through overlap and gives you the shortest list that actually covers your ground.

**You've spent hours researching and still don't know what to pick.**
→ A 5-minute briefing replaces hours of tab-hopping and comment-section research.

**You don't want another subscription.**

## Feature Bullets (plain language, not tech)

- **Takes 5 minutes to get your shortlist** — answer a focused briefing, not a 40-question survey
- **Ranked recommendations, not a random list** — each tool scored and explained against your actual goal
- **"Why this tool" on every pick** — no guessing why a tool was recommended
- **One first workflow to run today** — not a 12-step guide, just your next clear move
- **Session history** — go back and compare decisions without starting over
- **Saved preferences** — future runs are even faster
- **Works fully offline** — local fallback engine means you can get a useful answer without an API key
- **Optional OpenAI boost** — bring your own key for AI-powered ranking when you want it
- **Windows Credential Manager** — your API key is stored securely in your OS, not in the app
- **Export backup** — take your session history with you
