# Gumroad Product Description

---

## Tagline
Stop wasting money on AI tools that don't fit. Get a shortlist built for your actual goal — in minutes.

---

## Short Description
Windows desktop app that helps entrepreneurs, freelancers, and solo operators choose the right AI stack, understand why each tool fits, and run a first workflow today.

---

## Description

You've been there.

You're three YouTube videos deep into "the best AI tools for entrepreneurs." You have seven browser tabs open. Someone in the comments swears by a tool you've never heard of. Another says the one you just paid for is a waste.

Meanwhile, you haven't actually done the thing you needed to do.

**Navigator fixes that.**

### What it does

Answer a short briefing — your goal, budget, skill level, workflow style, and timeline. Navigator gives you:

- A ranked shortlist of the best AI tools for your specific situation
- A plain-language explanation of why each tool fits
- One clear first workflow to run today

### Why it works differently

Most "AI tool lists" are popularity contests. Navigator matches tools against what you're actually trying to accomplish, how much you want to spend, and how you like to work.

It runs locally on your Windows machine. No cloud account. No data leaves your device unless you deliberately add your own OpenAI key.

### What you get

- **Navigator.exe** — a packaged Windows desktop app
- Built-in recommendation engine with local fallback (works without an API key)
- Session history so you can compare decisions over time
- Saved preference profiles so future runs are even faster
- Optional OpenAI-powered ranking if you want it (bring your own key)

### Who this is for

- Freelancers and side-hustlers who need to move fast
- Entrepreneurs validating AI stacks for new projects
- Solo operators tired of tool bloat and subscription overlap
- Small business owners who want practical AI workflows without the enterprise complexity

### Who this isn't for

- People looking for a fully built AI business in a box
- Teams that need collaboration and enterprise features
- Anyone who wants a monthly subscription AI assistant
